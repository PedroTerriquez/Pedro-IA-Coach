# Design brief — base prompt for Claude Design

Goes at the top of every design request in Claude Design (the `design_prompt` skill adds it).
Short on purpose: the cards show the app, the prompt says only what they can't.
English; the app's copy stays in Spanish.

```
Coach Pedro: mobile gym app (iPhone PWA), portrait, dark mode only. Used mid-workout by
someone with sweaty fingers and under 10 seconds of attention per glance: one clear action
per screen in the bottom thumb zone, big readable numbers, touch targets ≥ 48 px and well
apart, read-only summaries with one way to edit. Keep the app's style from the existing cards
(bg #0a0a0a, lime accent #d4ff3a, Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono for numbers) and reuse its
components. UI copy in Spanish (Mexico). Always include an iPhone SE (320×568) version.
Don't design loading or offline screens.
```
